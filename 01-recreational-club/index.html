How Windrow works

01

Sessions that repeat

Each class runs for 10 weeks, then you re-enrol for the next session.

02

A card for every level

Every level has a skill card, stamped as your child learns each skill.

03

Parents can watch

The viewing gallery looks over the whole floor.

04

What to wear

Leotard or fitted shorts and t-shirt. Bare feet, hair tied back.

05

Re-enrolment week

Current families get first pick for one week before each session.

06

Make-up classes

Miss a class? Book a make-up in another class at the same level.
