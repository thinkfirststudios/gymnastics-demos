Questions families ask

Can we spread the cost?

Yes — the leotard and warm-up can be split over three months.

Can we skip a meet?

Yes; that meet's entry and coaching fee are not charged.

Is the leotard reusable?

Usually for two seasons.

What if we withdraw mid-year?

Set out in the withdrawal policy: club's counsel to supply.
