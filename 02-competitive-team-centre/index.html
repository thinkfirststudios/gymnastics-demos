About the programme

Five teams, from 9 to 20 hours a week. Entry is by tryout or evaluation — never by a sign-up button.

The commitment

Twelve months a year, three to five days a week. Every team's hours are on the Teams page.

The season

Meets from January to April: four to eight a season, two of them away.

What a team year involves

01

Tryouts

Two dates a year. A 90-minute session; families hear back within a week.

02

The training week

Vault, bars, beam and floor in rotation, then conditioning.

03

The annual cost

Fifteen lines, published, with the month each one falls due.

04

The meet season

January to April. Coaches travel with the team to away meets.

05

The booster

Parent organisation, separate from the club, with its own dues.

06

Watching training

The viewing gallery is open every evening.
